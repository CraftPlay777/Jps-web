A simple, ad-free map app made by CraftPlay777.

The code and license (MIT) are at github.com/CraftPlay777/Jps.

  • Optional map styles: © OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA, with SRTM data), CyclOSM, Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team and OpenStreetMap France.

  • Place photos and info: text from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) and photos from Wikimedia Commons, each with its own license. commons.wikimedia.org

  • Map library: osmdroid (Apache License 2.0).
  • Icons: Google Material Icons (Apache License 2.0).
