Jps

OpenStreetMap maps with no ads, no accounts and no API keys

Download APK
Android 6.0 or newer · Free · Open source (MIT)

Jps is a simple maps app with no ads, no accounts and no trackers. It uses OpenStreetMap maps and open services, so it needs no Google services or API keys.

Features

  • Place search with history and favorites
  • My location (GPS), zoom, scale and compass
  • Two-finger map rotation
  • Five map styles to choose from
  • Exact outline of the searched area
  • Country and province borders, with colors or flags
  • 3D globe with a relief mode
  • Directions: car, motorbike, truck, bike or on foot, with estimated time and steps
  • Photos and Wikipedia information in the place card
  • Share and copy coordinates
  • Four languages: Spanish, English, Chinese and French
  • Light, dark or system theme

Privacy first

No connection is made until you accept the privacy policy. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Privacy
